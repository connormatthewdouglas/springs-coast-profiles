The problem: busy owners, quiet profiles

On October 17, 2026 we looked at the public Google Maps listings of 35 home-service businesses from the Citrus County Chamber of Commerce directory (31 in Citrus County, 4 in Hernando). Here's what we saw.

22 of 34with a Google profile hadn't posted an update in the last 30 days. 16 had no posts visible at all.
22 of 31where we could see five recent reviews hadn't replied to all five newest.
5 of 31hadn't replied to any of their five newest reviews.
2 of 35had no claimed profile: one listing looked unclaimed and one business had no profile we could find.

These are good businesses, 14 of them with more than 100 reviews. They're just busy doing the actual work. A profile that hasn't been updated in months, or a review left without a reply, is a small missed chance to show customers you're active and paying attention.

This is a one-time check of public listings: one survey, one day, a small sample. It shows what we saw, not an industry statistic.

How it works: you stay in control

  1. A short written intake form (about 10 minutes, no call needed) covering your services, towns, hours and voice.
  2. You keep the Owner role and add us as a Manager on your profile. We never ask for your password, and you can remove us anytime from your profile settings.
  3. You sign a one-page consent form that spells out what we may post, confirms that review replies need your approval, and covers our no-fake-reviews pledge.
  4. The first week of drafts arrives in a simple shared doc. You approve, edit or drop each one. About 10 minutes a week.

FAQ

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No, never. You add us as a Manager from your profile's "People and access" settings. You keep the Owner role and can remove us anytime.

Will you post anything without my OK?

Review replies are always sent to you for approval first, unless you separately opt in, in writing, to auto-replies for 4- and 5-star reviews. Posts follow the schedule you approve, and every fact in them comes from you.

Will this get me to the top of Google Maps?

We can't promise that, and nobody honestly can. What we can do is keep your profile accurate, active and responsive, and show you the numbers from your own profile each month.

Can you get me more reviews?

We don't write, buy or filter reviews. We can help you set up an honest way to ask every customer for a review, with no gating.

What if my business doesn't have a profile, or it's unclaimed?

Claiming and verifying a profile is something only the owner can do. We'll send you a plain-English walkthrough of the steps for free.

Do you use AI?

Yes. Post and review-reply drafts are prepared with AI tools. A person reviews every draft before you see it, and you approve every review reply before it's posted. Posts go out only on the schedule you approve, and replies are never posted automatically unless you opt in to that in writing.

Are you affiliated with Google?

No. We're an independent local service, not a Google partner. "Google" and "Google Business Profile" are Google's trademarks.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month, and either side can cancel anytime by email. When you cancel, we stop all activity and remove ourselves as Manager within 2 business days, and you keep everything on your profile.

Do you have client results to show?

Not yet, and we won't make any up. Our own cleaning company's profile is our first test. We'll share real numbers, and founding clients' honest testimonials, only once we have them and only with permission.

What does the founding-client deal cost me?

Month 1 is free for our first 3 clients. In return you agree to give an honest testimonial in your own words (never a Google review) and let us share your before/after numbers from your own profile. No promised results, and you can cancel anytime.
